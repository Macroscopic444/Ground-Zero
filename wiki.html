FIELD GUIDE

Know the threats, events, loot and systems before they know you.

ZOMBIES

12+ VARIANTS

Ground Zero features twelve zombie classes, including a special Veteran class. Each class changes how a horde behaves.

Zombies only spawn in Dead City. Outside the city, the world is free of naturally spawning zombies.

🗡 SwarmerThe backbone of every horde. Standard base stats with no special abilities — Swarmers exist to overwhelm you with numbers.
⚡ RunnerFast and fragile. Runners close distance quickly but go down fast.
⛏ MinerDigs through your defenses. Miners break blocks between themselves and their target to reach you.
❤ NurseThe support unit. Nurses keep the horde alive — kill them first.
☠ SpitterRanged poison. Spitters hang back and lob acid at you.
🔥 ScorchedWreathed in flame. Fully fire-immune and unsettling to be near.
🗡 PsychopathThe berserker. Gets deadlier as it gets hurt.
🛡 TankA walking wall. High health, armored, hard to knock back.
🕸 WebberTraps you in place. Holds string and snares its victims.
💣 BursterThe suicide bomber. Detonates when it reaches you.
❄ FrostSlows you to a crawl. Wears a tell-tale aqua-dyed chestplate.
★ VeteranA zombie that has tasted blood. Created when a zombie kills a player — it gets promoted on the spot.
BLOOD MOON

EVENT NIGHT

Normal Blood Moons happen every 6 Minecraft days (2 real-life hours) and last 10 minutes.

Blood Moons cause zombies to spawn in huge hordes, turning the night into a major survival event.

Special Blood Moon Events will always be announced on the Discord. These events take place every Wednesday and Friday and will also refresh the loot throughout Dead City.

LOOT

DEAD CITY REWARDS

High-tier loot can only be found in Dead City. Hundreds of containers are waiting to be looted throughout the city, giving every player a reason to explore.

Loot is player-specific. If one player opens a chest and takes its contents, another player can later visit that same chest and loot it for themselves. One survivor searching a container does not permanently empty it for everyone else.

Special Blood Moon Events can refresh the loot throughout Dead City, so returning to the city can be worth the risk.

FACTIONS

BUILD YOUR OWN CLAN

You don't have to survive alone. Players can create their very own clans and invite people to join them. The system is completely player-driven through commands, letting survivors build their own groups, recruit members, and create their own alliances and rivalries.

Each clan also receives a shared Clan Chest that replaces the Ender Chest. With every new player added to your clan, three more slots become available in the Clan Chest, giving larger clans more shared storage.

For more information, visit the Commands page.

CHAT

GLOBAL & LOCAL CHAT

Players can switch between two chat channels. Global Chat is the public chat for everyone, while Local Chat is a proximity chat dedicated to RolePlay with a 50-block range.

Use /channel global for public chat or /channel local for RolePlay proximity chat.

ITEM FRAMES

HIDDEN DISPLAY

Crouch and right-click an item frame to toggle it between visible and invisible.

MASKED KILLER

HIDDEN IDENTITY

If you kill a player while you are invisible, the death message will display "Slain by masked killer" instead of your actual player name.

PLAYER SKULLS

TROPHIES

When you kill another player, their skull drops as a trophy for the winner.

GRAVES

THE DEAD LEAVE SOMETHING BEHIND

When a player dies, the server spawns a grave containing all of that player's items. The grave remains for 10 minutes. After that time, the stored items drop and can be looted by any player.

COMBAT LOG

15 SECOND COMBAT TIMER

Entering combat starts a 15-second timer. If you disconnect before the timer expires, the server automatically kills your player.
