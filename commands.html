Essential player commands for clans and roleplay chat.

/clan

/channel global

Global Chat

Switches you to the public Global Chat.

/channel local

Local Chat

Switches you to the Local Chat, a proximity chat dedicated to RolePlay. Local Chat has a 50-block range.
