COMMANDS

Player tools for PvP status and clan management.

/togglepvp

Toggle Player PVP

This allows the player to toggle their PVP on or off. If toggled on, the player can't deal damage or be harmed by another player. By pressing Tab, players will either have a red sword next to their name or a green peace sign depending on whether their PVP is enabled.

/clan

Clan System

Open the clan command system.

/clan create <name> <tag>

Create a Clan

Create your own player-driven clan with a name and tag.

/clan invite <player>

Invite a Player

Invite another survivor to your clan.

/clan help

Clan Help

View available clan commands.

/clan accept

Accept Invitation

Accept a pending clan invitation.

/clan leave

Leave a Clan

Leave your current clan.

/clan kick

Kick a Member

Remove a member from your clan when permitted.

/clan disband

Disband a Clan

Disband your player-created clan.
