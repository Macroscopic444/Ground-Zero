SERVER RULES

Survival is hard enough. Know the rules before you step into the city.

01

No Access to the End Dimension

No access to the End dimension.

  • No Elytras
  • Shulker Shells can be found in Dead City
02

Totems of Undying

  • Players are limited by server automation to only be able to carry one totem.
  • Totems can still be stored in shulker boxes.
  • Totems will not work in Dead City.
03

No Duping

No duping unless it's for a farm. Report any suspicious activity to an administrator.

04

Banned PVP Styles

  • Crystal PVP
  • Wither PVP
05

No Spawn Camping

  • Don’t kill players repeatedly near their spawn point or right after they respawn. Give people a chance to get back on their feet.
06

Respect Builds, Land, and Pets

Don’t mess with what others have made — that includes homes, farms, land, and pets. Killing someone’s animal is treated the same as griefing.

07

Don’t Abuse Loopholes

If you're doing something that clearly breaks the spirit of these rules, even if it's not written here, expect consequences. Use common sense.

08

This World Has No Police — So We All Keep the Peace

There are no admins babysitting every move. We rely on players to respect the rules and each other. If you want chaos, earn it — don’t cheap it.

09

Most Auto Farms Are Prohibited

Farms that are allowed include:

  • Iron Farms
  • Chicken / Wool Farms
  • Cobblestone Generator
  • Lava Farm
  • Bamboo / Sugar Cane Farms
  • XP Farm

This list may change overtime, but if the auto farm you wish to build is not listed here then assume you must ask a staff member in # ❓ Ask-The-Staff before you make it.

10

No Hacking

This includes:

  • Free Cam
  • X-Ray
  • Auto Clickers (in PVP)
  • Etc.

(Use of these could result in suspension or being banned from the server depending on the case!)
Report any suspicious activity to an administrator.

11

Combat Logging

  • This will result in dying upon leaving the game after a 15 second period of entering combat with our combat logging system.
12

No Alternative Accounts!

  • One account per player.
13

Loss of Items

  • Anti Combat Log & Grave plugins are present.
  • Parts of the Dead City sewers are open to the void.
  • Player may kill you if your /togglepvp is set to combat.

⚠️ Disclaimer ⚠️

In the event that your items are lost or stolen or destroyed, Staff will not return them.

Our staff team have certain commands to moderate in-game but do not have the ability to switch to creative or /give. Staff is just as much of a player as you are. Do not ask if we can TP, Return items, Help you reach your grave, or anything the average player couldn't do!

14

Respect the Staff Team

It is important to note that my staff team is to be treated with respect at all times and in turn, they should always treat you with respect as well. If there is a problem with an individual staff member please contact @Macroscopic_ immediately.

15

Staff Authority

In most cases staff members have the final say. They are an extension of me. Their word is my word in most scenarios.

16

Rules May Change

These rules may be adjusted and changed in the future. Any adjustments will be announced.
